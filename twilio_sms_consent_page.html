SMS Consent Documentation for Utility Crew Alerts

This page is intended to document the verbal opt-in process and sample messaging content for a worker notification system.

Program Description

This messaging program is used to notify utility workers and contractors about available storm restoration and related field work opportunities. Workers who choose to participate may receive text messages about job location, crew needs, rate information, reporting instructions, and related schedule updates.

Opt-In Type

Verbal opt-in

Workers are added only after they directly provide their mobile number and verbally agree to receive text messages for job alerts and work coordination.

Verbal Consent Script

When collecting consent, the caller or coordinator uses language substantially similar to the following:

Hello, this is Roger Jacobs. We can send you text messages about available utility and storm work opportunities, including location, crew needs, pay information, and schedule updates.

By agreeing, you consent to receive SMS messages from us at the mobile number you provide. Message frequency may vary depending on job activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

Do you agree to receive these text messages?

How Consent Is Collected

  1. A worker contacts the coordinator, or the coordinator speaks directly with the worker by phone or in person.
  2. The worker is told what types of text messages they will receive.
  3. The worker is told that message frequency may vary and that message and data rates may apply.
  4. The worker is told they can reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
  5. The worker verbally agrees and provides their mobile number.
  6. Only after that verbal agreement is the number added to the messaging list.

Sample Message

Roger Jacobs: Storm work available. Location: Kalamazoo, MI. Classification: Groundman. Pay Rate: $28/hr. Crews Needed: 4. Reply YES if available, STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Additional Information

  • These messages are intended for workers and contractors who requested job alert texts.
  • Message frequency varies based on staffing needs and storm activity.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Help Message Sample

Roger Jacobs: Utility crew alerts and work coordination. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.

Opt-Out Message Sample

Roger Jacobs: You have been unsubscribed and will no longer receive text messages from us. Reply START to resubscribe.
